## How to use these materials

**Before the workshop** — nothing is required. If you like, try asking any AI tool a question about an industry you know and notice what it gets right and wrong.

**During the workshop** — you will work in pairs and teams. One working AI account per pair is enough. Recommended tools: [Sikt KI-Chat](https://ki.sikt.no/nb/ai-chat) (log in with Feide) or Microsoft Copilot through your USN account; a personal tool is fine too.

**After the workshop** — read the course notes for the sessions you want to go deeper on, and compare your team's canvas and TRUST review with the worked examples.

Two honest warnings:

1. **The worked examples are one defensible answer, not the model answer.** Attempt your own canvas and TRUST review before reading them — they are far more useful as a comparison than as a template.
2. **Everything in the exercises is fictional.** Nordic Systems AS and Northstar Components AS do not exist, and all names, events, and figures are invented for teaching.

## Safe use

Use only the synthetic workshop material with AI tools. Never paste personal data, employer information, unpublished research, passwords, or anything confidential or security-sensitive into an AI service. You remain responsible for anything you submit.

## Three rules to keep

1. Start with the problem and the workflow — not the AI product.
2. Treat generated content as unverified until evidence says otherwise.
3. Scale only when value, responsibility, and controls scale together.
